    How to play and how to win

    The basics

    • Player 1 opens with any word.
    • Every next word must start with the last letter of the word before it. tiger → rabbit → tomato
    • No repeats. Once a word is in the chain it is gone for the game, and so is its plural or singular (cat blocks cats).
    • Run out of time and you lose a life. The next player gets the same letter.
    • Any mistake costs a life right away: wrong starting letter, a repeat, a word that's too short or not a real word. Turn this off in Rules to allow retries while the clock runs.
    • Last player with lives left wins.

    How to win

    • End on a trap letter. Few English words start with X, Q, Z, J, K or Y. Words like fox, jazz, ivy or kiosk hand your opponent a tough start.
    • Drain a letter. Each letter has a limited supply of easy words. Once the easy X or Y words are used, ending on that letter gets deadlier every time.
    • Avoid giving back easy letters. S, T, C, P and B start thousands of words. Ending there helps the next player.
    • Keep rescue words ready. Memorize two or three words for each hard letter: xylophone, quiz, zebra, yacht, jungle. Panic loses more games than vocabulary does.
    • Watch for bounce-backs. If the only answer you can think of ends on the same trap letter, you can send the trap straight back.
    • Challenge doubtful words. When a word can't be checked online, anyone can tap Challenge. A successful challenge costs the player who made it up a life.
